ENGLISH VERSION:

What is attached is a simplified version and starting point of something I built
for myself. It is a personal AI knowledge base — a system that captures
everything you learn, stores it in a database you own, and lets any AI read and
search it. Nobody can take it from you, change what it costs, or shut it down.
This version includes the benefit of a lot of hard lessons learned so you do not
have to learn them the hard way.

This build should take you somewhere between 9 and 17 hours spread across 8
levels. When you finish, you will have a framework that is state of the art, LLM
agnostic, and ready for your next AI build. More importantly, you will be ready
to begin building this into more advanced AI workflows — agents, scheduled
tasks, integrations, and pipelines that work for you around the clock.

THE MOST IMPORTANT INSTRUCTION IN THIS EMAIL:

Ask Claude everything. Do not ask me.

Not because I do not want to help. Because learning to ask the machine is the
actual skill you are here for. The brain you build is just the evidence.

You will hit words you have never seen, red error messages, and screens that do
not match the description. Every time, do the same thing: tell Claude exactly
what you are looking at and ask what to do. Paste the whole error. Paste a
screenshot — it can read those. You do not need the right vocabulary, and there
is no such thing as a stupid question.

The person who asks forty questions will be able to build the next thing alone.
The one who waits to be rescued will not. That is the entire point of the
exercise.

WHAT IT COSTS:

The build itself runs on Claude, so you need a paid Claude plan. The $20 USD per
month plan works if you spread this over a week or two. If you want to push hard
and finish in a couple of focused days, $100 USD per month is the better choice.
You can downgrade afterwards.

From Level 5 onward you will also need a separate AI API key, and this catches
almost everyone out: YOUR CLAUDE SUBSCRIPTION DOES NOT COVER IT. The
subscription pays for you talking to Claude. An API key pays for your software
calling an AI on its own. Two different products, two separate bills. When you
hit a payment page, nothing has gone wrong. Budget $5 to $10, which will
probably last you months — ongoing use is typically under a dollar a month.

Once built, this project is fully LLM agnostic. You are not locked into
Anthropic or anyone else.

HOW TO START:

Attached are 9 files. Open Level-0 first. Paste it into Claude. Claude will walk
you through everything. When Level-0 is done, open Level-1. Do them in order —
each level requires the previous one to be working, or the next one breaks.

You will need free accounts at github.com, supabase.com and vercel.com. Level-0
helps you set those up. Two more come later and take two minutes each: a free
Telegram account and a free supadata.ai key, both at Level 3.

This process works for most people, but computers differ and edge cases happen.
If something does not go as described, do not stop — tell Claude what is
happening, or paste a screenshot straight into the chat. Claude can see it and
will resolve it. That is the whole point of the process.

ONE MORE THING — IF YOU DO NOT WANT TO LEARN ALL THIS:

There is a second version that skips the teaching. Claude Code writes and
installs everything for you; you set up the accounts and watch. About three
hours instead of fifteen, and you end up with the same system — plus semantic
search and the auto-linking graph working out of the box.

    https://github.com/King-Tuerto/open-brain-express

Different goals. If you want to LEARN TO BUILD, use the levels attached here. If
you just want the THING WORKING, use that. Nobody will judge you, and you can
always come back and do it the long way afterwards.

I am not available for questions. Claude is your coach. Good luck.
